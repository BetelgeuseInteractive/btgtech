# 0008. Small custom material set

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11

## Context
Tier progression needs materials to gate recipes. Options ranged from vanilla-only materials to a large material system.

## Options Considered
- **A. Vanilla materials only:** least content; weak tech-mod feel and few progression gates.
- **B. Small custom set:** 2–3 new ores and 2 alloys (for example tin, lead, bronze, steel). Gives a tech-mod feel and progression gates with moderate cost.
- **C. Large material system:** many ores and alloys; high art and balancing cost.

## Decision
Option B. v1 adds 2–3 new ores and 2 alloys. The exact materials, their forms (ore, raw, ingot, nugget, dust, plate, storage block) and world generation are decided in planning topics 8 and 10.

## Consequences
- v1 includes ore world generation.
- Common item tags are used for materials so other mods' equivalent materials stay interchangeable (details in planning topic 12).
